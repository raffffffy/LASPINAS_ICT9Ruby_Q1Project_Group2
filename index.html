Welcome to OBMC located in the Philippines!

We are a progressive pre-K to 12-coeducational school, and we provide a strong alternative to international schools.

OBMC prepares students for life by blending traditional values with fresh innovation, and empowers them to become confident, self-driven, productive leaders capable of shaping a changing world.
