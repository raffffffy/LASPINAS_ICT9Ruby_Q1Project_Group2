Academic Programs

Montessori education tailored for every stage of growth.

Casa

Ages 3 to 5

Focuses on foundational motor skills, independence, sensory learning, and early social development in a prepared Montessori environment.

Primary Grade School

Ages 6 to 8

Builds core literacy, numeracy, and curiosity through hands-on Montessori materials and guided exploration.

Intermediate Grade School

Ages 9 to 12

Encourages critical thinking, research skills, and collaborative projects across science, math, language, and cultural studies.

Junior High School

Ages 13 to 16

Prepares adolescents for higher academics with deeper subject mastery, leadership opportunities, and real-world problem solving.

Senior High School

Ages 17 to 18

Equips students for college and career readiness through specialized academic tracks, research, and practical immersion.
